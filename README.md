👋 Welcome! I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Columbia University 🎓. I am lucky to be advised by Professor Baishakhi Ray and Professor Gail Kaiser.

I've worked on AI for **security applications**, such as **vulnerability detection**. I've also explored **code reasoning for code-related tasks**—specifically, investigating the techniques that underlie the ability of large language models to perform well on tasks like bug repair.

I've explored similar ideas in the context of **software engineering agents**, aiming to identify which components are critical for their performance and where future research should focus to improve agent capabilities.
